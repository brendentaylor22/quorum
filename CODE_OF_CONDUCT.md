# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Argue about the
code, not about the person writing it.

## Expected

- Respectful, direct technical discussion. Disagreement is welcome; contempt is
  not.
- Accepting that "no" is a complete answer to a feature request, and that a
  maintainer's time is finite.
- Respecting privacy — this project handles anonymous votes, and that ethic
  extends to its participants. Do not post anyone's personal information.

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by contacting the
maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions, and may block accounts,
for conduct judged inappropriate. This is a small project — enforcement is one
person exercising judgement, not a committee process.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to what one maintainer can honestly commit to.
